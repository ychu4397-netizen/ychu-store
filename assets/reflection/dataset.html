My Learning Experience

Using a tool recommended by my tutor, I collected data from public Reddit discussions. I found online forums useful because they provide accessible discussions where people's experiences and perspectives can be observed. However, I also realised that classifying the data into meaningful variables was much more challenging than I had expected. People's comments are diverse and often do not fit neatly into predefined categories. This experience made me reflect on the complexity of data analysis and the importance of making careful, consistent decisions when developing a coding framework.
